# Experiment 8: Charts, Graphs, and Data Mapping in Tableau

## Aim
To create bar, pie, line, and map-based views and select each view according to its analytical purpose.

## Theory
Bar charts compare categories, pie charts communicate a small part-to-whole relationship, line charts show ordered change, and maps display geographic variation. A professional view uses clear labels, restrained formatting, and a title that states the question being answered. Tableau's Show Me panel helps select a suitable chart from the chosen fields.

## Algorithm
1. Connect to the sales dataset.
2. Build a bar chart of Sales by Category.
3. Build a pie chart of Sales by Region.
4. Build a line chart of Sales by Quantity or date.
5. Build a map using City and Sales.
6. Add titles and verify that each chart communicates one clear message.

## Result
Multiple chart types were created and matched to their visualization purposes.
