# Experiment 10: Storytelling in Tableau

## Aim
To communicate data insights through a sequence of Tableau story points.

## Theory
Data storytelling combines evidence, structure, and explanation. A strong story moves from overview to comparison and then to a relationship or implication. Tableau Stories provide sequential story points, captions, and navigation so viewers can follow the analysis without losing context.

## Algorithm
1. Connect to the sales dataset.
2. Create Story Point 1: category performance.
3. Create Story Point 2: regional contribution.
4. Create Story Point 3: quantity and sales relationship.
5. Add a caption describing the insight at each point.
6. Arrange the points in logical order.
7. Navigate through the story and save it.

## Result
A three-point data story was designed to explain category performance, regional contribution, and the relationship between quantity and sales.
